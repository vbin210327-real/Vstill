隐私政策

生效日期及最近更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日

你的视频，由你掌控。Vstill 在你的设备上完成视频选帧、照片与实况照片生成，不会将你的媒体上传给开发者。

本政策适用于 Vstill iPhone App 及本隐私政策网页。Vstill 开发者（下称“我们”）通过 3196206659@qq.com 接收隐私咨询。

1. 我们处理哪些信息

  • 你选择的媒体：你从系统照片选择器或文件选择器导入的视频，包括其中的画面、音轨及文件内已有的元数据。App 仅处理你主动选择的文件，用于预览、选帧以及生成静态照片和实况照片。
  • 本地项目数据：导入视频的 App 内副本、项目封面、项目标识符、创建和更新时间、视频时长、文件大小及已收录的帧编号，以便你再次打开项目继续操作。
  • 本地偏好设置：导出类型、图片格式、实况时长及是否保留声音等设置。
  • 你主动发送的邮件：如果你联系我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你自愿提供的附件，用于回复问题和处理隐私请求。

媒体、项目和偏好设置均在设备本地处理和保存，我们无法远程读取。Vstill 无需注册账号，不集成广告、第三方分析或跨 App 跟踪 SDK，也不主动收集位置、通讯录、广告标识符或设备标识符。

2. 系统权限与媒体访问

导入视频：使用 Apple 提供的照片或文件选择器，由你决定分享哪些文件。Vstill 不请求浏览整个照片图库的权限。若所选文件储存在 iCloud 或其他文件提供方，系统可能需要通过相应服务下载文件，这由 Apple 或文件提供方及你的设置控制。

保存照片：导出时，App 会请求“添加照片”权限，将你选择生成的静态照片或实况照片写入系统相册。你可以在 iPhone“设置”中管理或撤销此权限；拒绝权限会使保存到相册的功能不可用。

声音：“保留声音”仅使用导入视频已有的音轨。App 不调用麦克风录音，也不调用相机拍摄。

3. 信息如何使用和共享

本地信息仅用于你发起的视频导入、播放、定位、帧收录、项目恢复和导出操作。Vstill 不会把这些信息传输到开发者服务器，也不会将其出售、出租或用于广告画像或 AI 模型训练。

你选择导出后，生成的媒体会交给 Apple 系统相册。若你启用了 iCloud 照片或设备备份，相应媒体和 App 数据可能由系统按照你的设置备份或同步；Vstill 没有自己的云同步服务。Apple 和其他由你选用的服务适用各自的隐私政策，例如 Apple 隐私政策。

对于你主动发送的支持邮件，我们仅用于沟通、解决问题和处理请求，不用于营销；除非取得你的授权、为处理邮件所必需的邮件服务处理，或法律要求，不向无关第三方披露。

4. 保存期限与删除方式

  • 项目：保留至你删除项目或删除 App。在主页长按项目并选择“删除项目”，即可删除该项目的本地视频副本、封面与选帧记录；相册或原文件位置的原视频不受影响。
  • 偏好设置：保留在本机，直至被新设置覆盖或随 App 数据删除。iOS 的“卸载 App”可能保留数据，“删除 App”才会移除其本机数据。
  • 临时文件：导出和预览过程中会产生临时文件。App 在相关流程完成、失败或预览关闭时进行清理，并在下次启动时清理其专用临时目录。
  • 已导出的媒体：保存在系统相册中，不随项目或 App 删除。你需要在“照片”App 中自行管理，包括“最近删除”中的内容。
  • 支持邮件：仅在处理咨询、解决问题和履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。你可以通过下方邮箱申请删除我们持有的通信内容。

删除本机数据不会自动删除已有的系统备份或其他服务中的副本，请在相应服务中管理。由于我们不持有你的本地媒体，我们无法代你远程恢复或删除这些文件。

5. 你的选择与请求

你可以停止使用 App、选择不导入文件、撤销照片添加权限、删除项目、删除 App，或自行删除相册中的导出结果。对于你发送给我们的邮件信息，你可以联系我们申请访问、更正或删除，以及行使适用法律赋予的其他权利。我们会在适用法律要求的期限内处理请求，并仅在必要时核实身份。

6. 安全与儿童隐私

Vstill 使用 iOS App 沙盒及系统媒体框架处理本地数据。请妥善保护设备及系统备份；任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

Vstill 不以收集儿童个人信息为目的，也不会主动向儿童索取个人信息。若你认为儿童通过支持邮件向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

7. 本网页的托管

本页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。我们未在页面中加入分析脚本、广告、跟踪像素或 Cookie，也未加载外部字体。访问本页面时，GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址等请求信息；这与 App 内的本地媒体处理不同。详情请参阅 GitHub Pages 说明及 GitHub 隐私声明。

8. 政策更新与联系

如果 App 功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及页面顶部的日期；重大变更会以适当方式提示，并在法律要求时取得同意。

隐私问题或请求，请联系 Vstill 开发者：3196206659@qq.com。请勿在邮件中发送与处理问题无关的敏感媒体。

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 19, 2026

Your videos stay under your control. Vstill selects video frames and creates photos and Live Photos on your device, without uploading your media to the developer.

This policy covers the Vstill iPhone app and this privacy policy website. The Vstill developer (“we”) can be contacted at 3196206659@qq.com.

1. Information we process

  • Media you select: Videos you import through the system Photos or Files picker, including their images, audio tracks, and existing embedded metadata. The app processes only files you choose, to preview videos, select frames, and create photos and Live Photos.
  • Local project data: An app-local copy of each imported video, a cover thumbnail, project identifiers, creation and update dates, duration, file size, and selected frame numbers, so you can resume your work.
  • Local preferences: Export type, image format, Live Photo duration, and whether to include the video's audio.
  • Email you send us: If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you voluntarily provide, to respond and handle your requests.

Media, projects, and preferences are processed and stored on your device; we cannot access them remotely. Vstill requires no account, includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and does not actively collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers.

2. Permissions and media access

Importing: Apple’s system Photos and Files pickers let you choose which files to share. Vstill does not request permission to browse your entire photo library. If a selected file is stored in iCloud or another file provider, the system may download it through that service, subject to your settings and the provider’s practices.

Saving: When exporting, the app requests add-only Photos permission to save the photos or Live Photos you create. You can manage or revoke this permission in iPhone Settings. Denying it prevents saving to Photos.

Audio: The “include audio” option uses only an imported video's existing audio track. Vstill does not record through your microphone or capture through your camera.

3. Use and sharing

Local information is used only for video import, playback, navigation, frame selection, project restoration, and exports that you initiate. Vstill does not transmit this information to developer servers, sell or rent it, or use it for advertising profiles or AI model training.

When you export, generated media is passed to Apple’s Photos library. If you enable iCloud Photos or device backups, media and app data may be synced or backed up by the system under your settings. Vstill has no separate cloud sync service. Apple and other services you choose have their own policies, including the Apple Privacy Policy.

Support emails are used only to communicate, resolve issues, and handle requests, not for marketing. We do not disclose them to unrelated third parties except with your authorization, as needed for email service processing, or as required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Projects: Retained until you delete the project or delete the app. On the home screen, long-press a project and choose Delete Project to remove its local video copy, cover, and frame selections. The original video in Photos or its source location is unaffected.
  • Preferences: Stored locally until replaced or removed with app data. iOS “Offload App” may keep app data; “Delete App” removes its on-device data.
  • Temporary files: Preview and export files are cleaned up as the relevant operation completes, fails, or the preview closes. The app also clears its dedicated temporary folder on its next launch.
  • Exported media: Remains in Photos after deleting a project or the app. Manage it in Photos, including Recently Deleted.
  • Support emails: Kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry, resolve issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence using the contact address below.

Deleting local data does not automatically remove existing backups or copies held by other services; manage those separately. Because we do not hold your local media, we cannot remotely recover or delete it for you.

5. Your choices and requests

You may stop using Vstill, decline to import files, revoke add-only Photos permission, delete projects, delete the app, or remove exported media from Photos. For information you send by email, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or exercise other rights under applicable law. We will handle requests within applicable legal deadlines and verify identity only when necessary.

6. Security and children’s privacy

Vstill uses the iOS app sandbox and system media frameworks to process local data. Protect your device and backups; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Vstill does not seek to collect children’s personal information or actively request it from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by support email, please contact us.

7. Website hosting

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or externally loaded fonts to this page. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses and other request information for security purposes when this website is accessed. This is separate from the app’s local media processing. See About GitHub Pages and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Updates and contact

We will update this policy and the date above if app features or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated appropriately, and consent obtained where required by law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Vstill developer at 3196206659@qq.com. Please do not email sensitive media unrelated to your request.
